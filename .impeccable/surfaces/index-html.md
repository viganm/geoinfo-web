# Surface brief: GeoInfo marketing site (index.html)

Scope: single-page marketing site, bilingual SQ (default) / EN. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Kosovo landowners, developers/architects, municipalities and public/donor clients. Action: call +383 44 239 065 or send a survey request; secondary: PTV licence/demo enquiry.
Proof on hand: 24 services in 8 groups, 4 traffic disciplines, official PTV Group dealer for Kosovo, partners PTV Group, Komuna e Prishtinës, Albi Commerce, Qeveria e Kosovës. No stats, projects, testimonials or team exist; do not invent them.
Memorable moment: the visitor's own city, mapped from real terrain, being surveyed live under their cursor.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a printed 1:25 000 topographic sheet of Prishtina drawn from real SRTM terrain and OpenStreetMap data; neatline, coordinate ticks, legend and margin furniture are the navigation and the content structure. It refuses the category default of a surveyor stock-photo hero over a blue icon-card service grid.

OWN-WORLD: Paper-white ground (#F6F7F4, never cream). Four print plates: planimetry black, contour bistre, hydro blue (the logo blue), vegetation green tint. Survey-flag orange is the only state and action colour. Archivo variable: wide caps for sheet titles, condensed spaced caps for place-name labels, italic for hydro-style accents. Martian Mono only for coordinates and measurements. Hairline rules, double neatline, tick marks, leader lines and scale bars. No cards, no decorative shadows.

STORY: Visitors see their own city mapped precisely, then understand that GeoInfo measures land (the legend lists 8 groups and 25 services), models traffic as the official PTV dealer, and supports real estate and architecture. The partners earn their trust, and they call or send a survey request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The full viewport is one sheet. The top margin holds the wordmark on the left, the index nav, the SQ/EN switch and an orange "Kërko ofertë" button. A double neatline carries KOSOVAREF01 E/N ticks. The plate shows contours, water, forest, roads and buildings. The headline "Vizion i qartë." sits at about 7vw over the flat Kosovo plain on the left. A live traverse runs at the city: a station symbol fires rays while the readout at the bottom right resolves Hz/V/SD, then E/N/H, digit by digit. The bottom margin holds the scale bar, contour interval, projection and attribution.
Signature interaction: a cursor reticle with a live E/N/H readout over the real terrain. Hovering a legend entry draws a leader line to its symbol.

FORM: The Map Sheet, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 4a248205.
