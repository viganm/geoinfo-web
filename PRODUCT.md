## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + vanilla TypeScript, GSAP/ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll, canvas/SVG for survey/traffic/architecture animation. Static build, host-agnostic (Netlify, Vercel, cPanel). Chosen by the user from a recommendation.

## Users

- Landowners, families and buyers in Kosovo who need a boundary, topographic, cadastral or subdivision survey, often tied to a sale, inheritance, dispute or building permit.
- Developers, contractors and architects who need site layout/staking, as-built and monitoring surveys, feasibility studies and parcel planning.
- Municipalities, public agencies and infrastructure clients (roads, utilities, hydro) who need geodetic, GIS, orthophoto and traffic studies, including donor/international-funded projects that evaluate suppliers in English.

They arrive asking "can this firm measure my land / site / network correctly, on time, and stand behind it legally?"

## Product Purpose

GeoInfo Sh.P.K. is a Kosovo engineering firm: surveying and geodesy first, plus real-estate/development support, architecture, telecommunications and traffic engineering (planning, modelling, simulation). The website must establish trust in precision and expertise, explain the breadth of services, and turn visitors into enquiries (phone, email, contact form).

## Positioning

A single partner that both measures the ground (geodesy, cadastre, drones, 3D laser scanning, GIS) and models what moves on it (PTV VISUM / VISSIM traffic modelling, ITS, road safety). Tagline in use: "Vizion i Qartë, GeoInfo." / "Partneri juaj i Besueshëm në Inxhinieri."

## Operating Context

Field instruments (total stations, GNSS receivers, drones, laser scanners), cadastral offices and municipal permit processes in Kosovo, legal boundary disputes, PTV VISUM/VISSIM traffic models. Clients usually make first contact by phone.

## Product Principles

1. Precision is the brand: every number, line and alignment on the page should look measured.
2. Surveying leads; traffic, real estate and architecture are shown as extensions of knowing the ground.
3. Make the path to a phone call or enquiry obvious from every section.
4. Speak plainly to landowners and credibly to engineers and public clients, in both languages.
